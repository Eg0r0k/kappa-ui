New `PageIndicator`: marks the current page among `count` as dots, a stretched pill or line segments, with a `PageIndicatorItem` per page. `v-model:page` counts from 1; five sizes, any tone, both orientations; `cumulative` and `progress` fill story bars and autoplay; `readonly` turns it into a labelled picture; `touchTarget` grows the pressable areas. The items are buttons with roving focus.
