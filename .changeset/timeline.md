New `Timeline`: a record of events with an optional current one. Eight parts (`Timeline`, `TimelineItem`, `TimelineIndicator`, `TimelineSeparator`, `TimelineContent`, `TimelineDate`, `TimelineTitle`, `TimelineDescription`), `v-model`/`default-value` for the current record, `reverse` for lists that run newest first, `orientation`, `size` from `xs` to `xl` as the record's density through `--timeline-*` variables on the root, and `color` on the root or on one indicator.
