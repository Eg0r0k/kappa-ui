ColorPicker: `ColorPicker`, `ColorPickerArea`, `ColorPickerSlider`, `ColorPickerField`, `ColorPickerPreview`, `ColorPickerSwatches` and `ColorPickerSwatch` over Reka UI's colour primitives. One `v-model` string in `hex`, `rgb`, `hsl` or `hsb` feeds every part; the area, the sliders and the swatches work from the keyboard and read out as sliders and options; the sliders are drawn on `Slider`'s parts with its `variant`, `touch-target`, sizes, halo and press scale, one `size` from `xs` to `xl` scales the parts together, and a surrounding `Field` supplies the label, description, error and disabled state.
