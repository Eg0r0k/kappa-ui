New `ErrorState`: Empty's anatomy for a failure. Six parts (`ErrorState`, `ErrorStateHeader`, `ErrorStateMedia`, `ErrorStateTitle`, `ErrorStateDescription`, `ErrorStateActions`) over the Empty parts, `role="alert"` on the root unless you pass another `role`, a circled exclamation mark in the destructive colour as the default media, and `size` from `xs` to `xl` as on Empty.
