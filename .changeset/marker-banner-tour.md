New `Marker`: an inline status, a bordered row or a labelled separator, from shadcn-vue, with a `color`. New `Banner`: a full-width notice with a title, a subtitle, actions and a close button, `v-model:open` and `color`. New `Tour`: parts over Popover for a guided tour driven by `useTour`. `Skeleton` takes a `color`.
