Splitter: `Splitter`, `SplitterPanel` and `SplitterHandle` over Reka UI's splitter. The handle is a `line` or a `gutter` that lights up in `--primary` while the pointer is in its hit zone, with an optional `grip`; double-clicking it puts the panels beside it back to their starting sizes.
