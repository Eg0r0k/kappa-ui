`AlertClose`: a close button for `Alert`, like `BannerClose`. It sits at the end of the title's line (centred when horizontal), takes the alert's text colour and a size that follows the alert's, and the text keeps clear of it. `Alert` gains `v-model:open`; the item now depends on `button` and `@lucide/vue`.
