ScrollArea scrolls on both axes with `orientation="both"`, emits `reachEdge` once per entry into an edge zone (`edgeOffset` widens it), and ships `useInfiniteScroll` and `InfiniteScroll`: loading on approach to any of four edges with a promise, `'stop'` to end a direction, initial fill of the viewport and a kept reading position when content is prepended.
