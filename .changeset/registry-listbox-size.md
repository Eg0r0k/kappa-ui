`Listbox` takes `size`, from `xs` to `xl` (default `md`), and sets `data-size`. Options are 28 to 48px tall and their padding, gap, icons and text follow the control scale, like a menu of the same size. At `md` the gap between an icon and its label is now 8px instead of 12px.
