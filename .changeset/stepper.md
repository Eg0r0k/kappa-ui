Stepper: `Stepper`, `StepperItem`, `StepperTrigger`, `StepperIndicator`, `StepperTitle`, `StepperDescription` and `StepperSeparator` over Reka UI's stepper, shaped after shadcn-vue. The indicator and the separator colour themselves from the step's state, the stepper is linear by default, and one `size` from `xs` to `xl` scales the indicator, its icon, the type, the gaps and the separator.
