## Touch targets

Fingers are bigger than cursors. Anything you tap should be at least 48px, and most controls are smaller. `touch-target` fixes that without changing how they look:

```vue
<Button size="sm" touch-target="expand">Ok</Button>
```

- `expand`: the tappable area grows invisibly. Nothing moves, but neighbours closer than 48px overlap, and a tap can hit the wrong one. Good for controls that stand alone.
- `wrapper`: also reserves the space with a margin. No overlap, but the control takes more room, and whatever sits next to it moves along. Good for tight rows and lists.

Three rows of 32px buttons, 4px apart. Pink is where a tap lands. Where it gets darker, two areas overlap, and the tap is a coin toss.

::component-preview{name="button-touch-target-row"}
::

A list works the same way, only vertically: `expand` keeps the rows tight and stacks the areas, `wrapper` spreads the rows apart and the labels move with the boxes.

::component-preview{name="checkbox-touch-target-list"}
::

It's off by default: in dense layouts, it does more harm than good.

Button, Badge, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Slider, Rating, ColorPicker's sliders and PageIndicator take it. The outlines in the demos only show the area. They aren't part of the component.
