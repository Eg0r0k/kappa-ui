## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { ref } from 'vue'

import {
  ColorPicker,
  ColorPickerArea,
  ColorPickerField,
  ColorPickerPreview,
  ColorPickerSlider,
} from '@/components/ui/color-picker'

const color = ref('#3b82f6')
</script>

<template>
  <ColorPicker v-model="color" class="w-64">
    <ColorPickerArea />
    <ColorPickerSlider channel="hue" />
    <ColorPickerSlider channel="alpha" />
    <div class="flex gap-(--color-picker-gap)">
      <ColorPickerPreview />
      <ColorPickerField />
    </div>
  </ColorPicker>
</template>
```

`ColorPicker` holds one colour and hands it to every part inside it; the parts are Reka UI's colour primitives with kappa-ui's styles, laid out however you like. The root is a `role="group"` with no width of its own, so give it one with `class`; `size` sets the height of the area and the size of the sliders, the field, the preview and the swatches.

`v-model` is a string. On the way out it is written in `format`: `hex` by default, with an eight-digit hex once the alpha drops below one, or `rgb`, `hsl` and `hsb`. On the way in any of those is accepted, three, six or eight hex digits included. A string that does not parse is ignored and the picker keeps its colour.

`ColorPickerArea` is a saturation and brightness plane under the current hue; the thumb is a `role="slider"` with two values and moves with the arrows, ten steps at a time with Shift. `ColorPickerSlider` moves one `channel`, `hue` by default, and draws that channel's gradient through the current colour; `alpha` gets a checkerboard underneath. `ColorPickerField` edits the colour as hex, or one channel as a number with `channel`, and commits on Enter or blur; an invalid entry reverts. `ColorPickerPreview` shows the colour over a checkerboard. `ColorPickerSwatches` and `ColorPickerSwatch` are a listbox of presets; the swatch matching the colour is marked, and the check takes black or white from the swatch's own contrast.

### Chooser

### Channels

Sliders and fields for `red`, `green`, `blue` and `alpha` instead of an area; the parts compose into any picker.

::component-preview{name="color-picker-channels"}
::

### Formats

`format` picks how the model is written; changing it rewrites the model at once.

::component-preview{name="color-picker-formats"}
::

### Sizes

`size` goes from `xs` (96px area) to `xl` (224px) and scales every part with it.

::component-preview{name="color-picker-sizes"}
::

### Field

Inside a [Field](/docs/components/field) the group takes its label, the parts take the description and error as their `aria-describedby`, the field input turns `aria-invalid`, and `disabled` and `required` follow the Field. `name` adds a hidden input for the form.

::component-preview{name="color-picker-field"}
::

### Keyboard

::keyboard-table
---
rows:
  - keys: ArrowLeft, ArrowRight
    description: Moves the area thumb along its horizontal channel, or a horizontal slider by one step.
  - keys: ArrowUp, ArrowDown
    description: Moves the area thumb along its vertical channel, a vertical slider by one step, or a field's value by one step.
  - keys: Shift + Arrow
    description: Moves the area thumb by ten steps.
  - keys: PageUp, PageDown
    description: Moves a slider or a field by ten steps.
  - keys: Home, End
    description: Jumps a slider or a field to its minimum or maximum; jumps the area thumb to the horizontal ends.
  - keys: Enter
    description: Commits the field's text, or selects the focused swatch.
  - keys: Space
    description: Selects the focused swatch.
---
::
