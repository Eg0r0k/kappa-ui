## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import {
  NavigationMenu,
  NavigationMenuContent,
  NavigationMenuItem,
  NavigationMenuLink,
  NavigationMenuList,
  NavigationMenuTrigger,
} from '@/components/ui/navigation-menu'
</script>

<template>
  <NavigationMenu aria-label="Main">
    <NavigationMenuList>
      <NavigationMenuItem value="products">
        <NavigationMenuTrigger>Products</NavigationMenuTrigger>
        <NavigationMenuContent>
          <NavigationMenuLink href="/analytics">Analytics</NavigationMenuLink>
          <NavigationMenuLink href="/reports">Reports</NavigationMenuLink>
        </NavigationMenuContent>
      </NavigationMenuItem>
      <NavigationMenuItem>
        <NavigationMenuLink href="/pricing">Pricing</NavigationMenuLink>
      </NavigationMenuItem>
    </NavigationMenuList>
  </NavigationMenu>
</template>
```

`NavigationMenu` is Reka UI's navigation menu with kappa-ui's styles: a `nav` landmark of top-level items. An item holds either a trigger and its panel, or a single link. Panels open on hover after a short delay, on click and from the keyboard, and all of them open in one viewport under the list, which resizes and slides as you move between triggers.

It's for site navigation: links to pages. For a list of actions, use [Menu](/docs/components/menu); for switching panels in place, [Tabs](/docs/components/tabs).

`NavigationMenuLink` styles itself by where it sits. At the top level it looks like a trigger; inside a panel it becomes a block with room for a title and a description under it. `active` marks the current page with `aria-current="page"`.

Give the `nav` an `aria-label` when the page has more than one: screen readers list landmarks by name.

## Examples

### Sizes

`size` goes from `xs` (28px) to `xl` (48px) and scales the items, the panel links, their text and icons.

### Link variant

`variant="link"` drops the hover fill and underlines instead, for a lighter header. The current page stays underlined. A `disabled` link stays focusable and announced, but a click does nothing.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-link-variant" height="16rem" align="start"}
::

### Indicator

`NavigationMenuIndicator` inside the list draws an arrow under the open trigger that follows it from trigger to trigger.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-indicator" height="16rem" align="start"}
::

### Align

`align` lines the viewport up with the start, centre or end of the open trigger. `start` and `end` follow the reading direction, so under `dir="rtl"` `start` is the right edge. Near the edge of the page the viewport moves back in, 10px from it.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-align" height="18rem" align="start"}
::

### Without a viewport

With `:viewport="false"`, each panel opens under its own trigger instead of in a shared box, and there's no slide between them. Panels can hold anything, a form included: Space, Enter and the arrow keys work as usual in a text field, and the form submits like any other. Close the menu after a submit through `v-model`.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-no-viewport" height="20rem" align="start"}
::

### Controlled

`v-model` holds the `value` of the open item, or an empty string when none is. Set it to open a panel from elsewhere, or clear it to close.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-controlled" height="20rem" align="start"}
::

### Click only

Hover menus suit a marketing site. In an app, where a panel that opens as the pointer passes by gets in the way, `disable-hover-trigger` opens panels on click only and `disable-pointer-leave-close` keeps them open until a click outside or Escape.

A link inside a panel that starts with an icon lays the icon and text out in a row.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-click-only" height="16rem" align="start"}
::

### Your own links

`as-child` hands a link's attributes, classes and behaviour to your router's link, so a click changes the route without reloading the page and still closes the menu.

```vue
<NavigationMenuLink as-child :active="route.path === '/pricing'">
  <RouterLink to="/pricing">Pricing</RouterLink>
</NavigationMenuLink>
```

`NuxtLink` works the same way. A plain `<a>` around a `NavigationMenuLink` doesn't: the menu wouldn't know it was chosen and stays open.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-as-child" height="16rem" align="start"}
::

### Keep panels mounted

By default a closed panel's content is removed from the page. With `:unmount-on-hide="false"`, every panel stays in the page, hidden, so its links are there for crawlers and the browser's find in page.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-keep-mounted" height="14rem" align="start"}
::

### Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the items in a column, and panels open beside it. ArrowUp and ArrowDown move between items, and ArrowRight (ArrowLeft in right-to-left text) enters an open panel.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-vertical" height="20rem" align="start"}
::

### Responsive

A row of hover panels doesn't work on a phone. Swap it for a [Drawer Menu](/docs/components/drawer-menu) under a breakpoint, with the same links in submenus.

::component-preview{name="navigation-menu-responsive" height="18rem" align="start"}
::

## Fixed here

Reka UI's navigation menu has three bugs, still there in 2.11, that kappa-ui's parts work around. Once they are fixed upstream, the workarounds do nothing.

- Reka finds the open trigger by checking whether its id contains the open value, so values such as `docs` and `docs-api`, or a short one like `a`, opened the wrong trigger's panel and indicator. Item values are now wrapped before they reach Reka, and `v-model` still sees your own.
- In right-to-left text, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight moved the wrong way between top-level items. `NavigationMenuList` swaps them back.
- Without a viewport, Space anywhere in a panel closed the menu, so it couldn't be typed into a field or press a button. `NavigationMenuContent` keeps Space inside the panel. The side effect: a Space keydown inside such a panel no longer reaches listeners on the document.

## Known limits

- The viewport lives inside the `nav`, not in a portal. A header with `overflow: hidden` clips it, and a sticky header needs its own `z-index`, as the panel can't rise above its header's stacking context. The upside: CSS variables set on the header reach the panel. See [nuxt/ui#5439](https://github.com/nuxt/ui/issues/5439).
- Keep the list on one line. On a list that wraps, the viewport opens under the whole list rather than under the trigger's row ([reka-ui#1522](https://github.com/unovue/reka-ui/issues/1522)). Switch to a drawer before it gets that narrow.
- Don't put a navigation menu in a [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog) or [Drawer](/docs/components/drawer): the focus trap and the menu disagree and Tab skips the last link ([reka-ui#1782](https://github.com/unovue/reka-ui/issues/1782)). Use a [Drawer Menu](/docs/components/drawer-menu) there.
- Once a panel is open, moving to another trigger or out of the menu waits 150ms, and a click right after a hover opens the panel is ignored for 300ms. Neither can be changed ([reka-ui#1578](https://github.com/unovue/reka-ui/issues/1578)); `delay-duration` and `skip-delay-duration` only set the first opening.
- Trigger and link must be direct children of their item. Wrapped in a tooltip or a `span`, the arrow keys skip them.
- `orientation` is read once, so the menu remounts and closes when it changes.
- Arrow keys pressed on top-level items don't reach keydown listeners on the document ([reka-ui#2400](https://github.com/unovue/reka-ui/issues/2400)).
- On reka-ui 2.10, an open trigger is followed by a focusable element hidden with `aria-hidden`, which axe reports as `aria-hidden-focus`. Reka 2.11 fixes it ([reka-ui#2163](https://github.com/unovue/reka-ui/issues/2163)).
- No submenus inside panels yet, and only one panel open at a time. For a sidebar with several open groups, use [Accordion](/docs/components/accordion).

## API Reference

Every part takes a `class` that merges with its own. On `NavigationMenuList` it lands on the `ul`.

::api-reference{parts="NavigationMenu,NavigationMenuList,NavigationMenuItem,NavigationMenuTrigger,NavigationMenuContent,NavigationMenuLink,NavigationMenuIndicator,NavigationMenuViewport"}
::

`navigationMenuTriggerStyle({ size, variant })` returns a trigger's classes, to give your own element the same look.

### Data attributes

::data-slots
---
rows:
  - slot: navigation-menu
    attributes: data-size, data-variant, data-viewport, data-orientation
    description: The `nav`. `data-viewport` is `true` or `false`.
  - slot: navigation-menu-list
    attributes: data-orientation
    description: The `ul` of top-level items.
  - slot: navigation-menu-item
    description: An `li`.
  - slot: navigation-menu-trigger
    attributes: data-state, data-disabled, aria-expanded
    description: The button that opens a panel. `data-state` is `open` or `closed`.
  - slot: navigation-menu-content
    attributes: data-state, data-motion, data-orientation
    description: A panel. `data-motion` is `from-start`, `from-end`, `to-start` or `to-end` while it slides in or out as you switch triggers.
  - slot: navigation-menu-link
    attributes: data-active, aria-current, aria-disabled
    description: A link, at the top level or in a panel.
  - slot: navigation-menu-indicator
    attributes: data-state, data-orientation
    description: The arrow under the open trigger. `data-state` is `visible` or `hidden`.
  - slot: navigation-menu-viewport-wrapper
    description: The positioned box the viewport sits in.
  - slot: navigation-menu-viewport
    attributes: data-state, data-orientation
    description: The shared box panels open in. Carries `--reka-navigation-menu-viewport-width`, `-height`, `-left` and `-top`.
---
::

## Accessibility

It follows the WAI disclosure navigation pattern, not a menu: the root is a `nav` landmark, each trigger a `button` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and each panel is labelled by its trigger. Links are ordinary links, so screen readers list them with the page's other links. Focus is never trapped.

::keyboard-table
---
rows:
  - keys: Tab
    description: Moves through the top-level items. From an open trigger, moves into its panel, and past the panel's last link on to the next item.
  - keys: Enter / Space
    description: Opens or closes the focused trigger's panel, or follows the focused link.
  - keys: ArrowDown
    description: On an open trigger, moves into its panel. In a vertical menu, the key is ArrowRight, or ArrowLeft in right-to-left text.
  - keys: → ←
    description: Moves to the next or previous top-level item, without wrapping. In right-to-left text they swap.
  - keys: ↓ ↑
    description: Moves to the next or previous top-level item; inside a panel, to the next or previous link, except in a text field.
  - keys: Home / End
    description: Moves to the first or last top-level item.
  - keys: Escape
    description: Closes the panel and returns focus to its trigger.
---
::
