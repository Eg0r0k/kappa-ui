## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { Field, FieldLabel } from '@/components/ui/field'
import { Radio, RadioGroup } from '@/components/ui/radio-group'

const density = ref('comfortable')
</script>

<template>
  <RadioGroup v-model="density" aria-label="Density">
    <Field v-for="option in ['default', 'comfortable', 'compact']" :key="option" orientation="horizontal">
      <Radio :value="option" />
      <FieldLabel>{{ option }}</FieldLabel>
    </Field>
  </RadioGroup>
</template>
```

`RadioGroup` and `Radio` are Reka UI's radio group and item with kappa-ui's styles. They work like the [checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox) and share its sizes, touch targets, state layer and group layouts; this page covers what is different about radios.

The look follows Material's radio button: a 2px ring that turns `--primary` when selected, and a dot that grows in from the centre over 300ms with the `emphasized-decelerate` easing. With reduced motion it appears at once.

The group is a single tab stop. Tab lands on the selected radio, or on the first when none is, and the arrow keys move the selection, as the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern describes. With `orientation="horizontal"`, left and right move it too.

`variant` and `orientation` lay the options out as cards, one bordered list or a table, in a column or a row; they come from [Choice Group](/docs/components/choice-group).

### Invalid

A radio is valid or invalid as a group: the error is that nothing, or the wrong thing, is selected. Put `invalid`, `required` and the `FieldError` on the `FieldSet`. The group becomes `aria-invalid` and `aria-required` and is described by the error, and every radio in it and the legend turn red; the individual radios stay without `aria-invalid`, which ARIA does not define for them. Below, press Continue without choosing a size; Reset clears the choice, since a radio cannot be unselected by clicking it again.
