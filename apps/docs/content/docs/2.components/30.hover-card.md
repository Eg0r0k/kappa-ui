## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { Button } from '@/components/ui/button'
import { HoverCard, HoverCardContent, HoverCardTrigger } from '@/components/ui/hover-card'
</script>

<template>
  <HoverCard>
    <HoverCardTrigger as-child>
      <Button variant="link">@kappa-ui</Button>
    </HoverCardTrigger>
    <HoverCardContent>A preview of what the link leads to.</HoverCardContent>
  </HoverCard>
</template>
```

`HoverCard` is shadcn-vue's hover card, driven by `@kappa-ui/core/hover-card` and drawn on the same surface as a popover. On a desktop it opens when the pointer rests on the trigger or the trigger takes keyboard focus, and closes when both have left, after `closeDelay`. It is for a preview of what a link leads to, content a reader can do without, so screen readers do not announce it.

On touch it follows the trigger. A link, or anything else with a native long press, opens the card on a long press of `touchDelay`, and a tap keeps doing what it did: the link navigates. A button or a plain element opens on a tap and closes on the next one. Once open by touch the card stays while the page scrolls, and closes on a tap outside it or on Escape. `touch="long-press"` asks for the long press everywhere; `touch="off"` leaves touch alone.

`update:open` reports why the card opened or closed: `trigger-hover`, `trigger-focus`, `trigger-press`, `outside-press`, `escape-key`, `scroll` or `disabled`.

## Examples

### Delays

`openDelay` and `closeDelay` set how long, in milliseconds, the pointer has to rest before the card opens and how long it stays after the pointer leaves the trigger and the card; 700 and 300 by default.
