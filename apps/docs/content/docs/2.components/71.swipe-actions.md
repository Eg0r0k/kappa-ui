## Usage

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
import { SwipeAction, SwipeActions, SwipeContent, SwipeItem, SwipeRoot } from '@/components/ui/swipe-actions'
</script>

<template>
  <SwipeRoot as="ul">
    <SwipeItem as="li">
      <SwipeActions side="end" full-swipe>
        <SwipeAction color="destructive" @click="remove">Delete</SwipeAction>
      </SwipeActions>
      <SwipeContent>Grace Hopper</SwipeContent>
    </SwipeItem>
  </SwipeRoot>
</template>
```

Drag a row sideways and the strip on that side slides in with it. Released past half the strip, or on a quick flick, the row stays open on the strip; otherwise it springs back. Tap an action to run it and close the row; tap the row, press anywhere else or press `Escape` to close it. With `close-on-scroll`, any scroll closes it too. Mouse, pen and touch all drag; on a touch screen a vertical drag still scrolls the list. A closed row does not move towards a side without actions, so that swipe reaches whatever is around the row, such as a side drawer or [Swipe Views](/docs/components/swipe-views).

`side` is `start` or `end` in the reading direction, so the strips swap places in right-to-left text. A row takes up to one strip per side. Actions sit from the content outwards in the order you write them, each as wide as its content, at least 80px.

With `full-swipe` on a strip, dragging past half the row arms its outermost action: it floods the row and `data-armed` is set on the strip and the action. Released armed, the action is clicked, so a tap and a full swipe reach the same handler.

Inside a `SwipeRoot`, opening a row closes the one that was open. The root renders nothing of its own; `as="ul"` with `as="li"` on the items makes the list.

Every part accepts `class`, merged over its base classes.

::api-reference{parts="SwipeItem,SwipeActions,SwipeAction,SwipeContent,SwipeRoot"}

### Data attributes

::data-slots
---
rows:
  - slot: swipe-root
    description: The list whose rows close one another.
  - slot: swipe-item
    attributes: data-state, data-disabled, data-dragging
    description: A row. `data-state` is `closed`, `start` or `end`.
  - slot: swipe-actions
    attributes: data-side, data-armed
    description: A strip of actions. `data-armed` is set while a full swipe is armed on its side.
  - slot: swipe-action
    attributes: data-color, data-armed
    description: An action, filled with its colour. `data-armed` is set on the outermost action while a full swipe is armed.
  - slot: swipe-action-content
    description: The visible slice of an action, its icon and label.
  - slot: swipe-content
    attributes: data-dragging
    description: The layer that moves with the drag.
---
::

## Accessibility

Actions are buttons named by their label. A closed strip is `inert`, so its actions are out of the tab order and the accessibility tree until the row opens. From the row's focused content, the arrow keys open and close the strips. A swipe is hard to discover with a screen reader, so offer the same actions in another place too, such as a [menu](/docs/components/menu) on the row.

::keyboard-table
---
rows:
  - keys: ArrowLeft
    description: Moves the content left, opening the strip on the right or closing the one on the left.
  - keys: ArrowRight
    description: Moves the content right, opening the strip on the left or closing the one on the right.
  - keys: Escape
    description: Closes the open row.
---
::
